# NASCAR Stock Car & Rallycross Reference Dossier

## NASCAR Stock Cars
### 1. Chevrolet Monte Carlo Street Stock (`nascar_monte_carlo_ss`)
- **Silhouette:** Boxy 1980s G-body American muscle silhouette, upright notchback rear window, flat hood and trunk deck.
- **Details:** Steel tubular front/rear crash bars, Hoosier white-letter racing tires, driver side window net.
- **Livery:** Blue #24 with yellow and red grassroots racing graphics.

### 2. Ford Mustang Street Stock (`nascar_mustang_street_stock`)
- **Silhouette:** 1990s Fox-body notchback coupe silhouette, sloped front grille, muscular haunches.
- **Details:** Steel bumpers, window net, low decklid spoiler.
- **Livery:** Red with white hood stripes and race number plate.

### 3. Super Late Model Camaro (`nascar_super_late_model`)
- **Silhouette:** Low-slung asymmetric tubular chassis, low wedge nose with front splitter, high rear quarter panels.
- **Details:** Driver window net, aggressive tall rear spoiler blade.
- **Livery:** Neon Orange and Black short-track scheme.

### 4. Chevrolet SS ARCA Spec (`nascar_arca_chevy_ss`)
- **Silhouette:** Gen-6 NASCAR stock car profile with smooth aerodynamic roofline and rounded rear window.
- **Details:** Roof escape flaps, tall clear Lexan rear spoiler, low front splitter.
- **Livery:** Blue and Yellow ARCA Menards Series racing scheme.

### 5. Chevrolet Silverado RST Truck (`nascar_silverado_truck`)
- **Silhouette:** NASCAR Craftsman Truck: upright pickup truck cab, flat bed cover / tonneau, tall rear spoiler.
- **Details:** Front truck grille graphic, roof air deflector fins, boom-tube side exhaust.
- **Livery:** Red and Black Chevy Silverado RST racing graphics.

### 6. Chevrolet Corvette C7 TA1 (`nascar_corvette_ta1`)
- **Silhouette:** Trans-Am TA1 silhouette: low Corvette C7 body lines over tubular spaceframe.
- **Details:** Massive side boom-tube exhaust in front of rear wheel, giant high-mount carbon rear wing.
- **Livery:** Yellow with black TA1 racing accents.

## Rallycross & All-Terrain
### 7. Peugeot 208 Rally4 (`rally_peugeot_208_rally4`)
- **Silhouette:** Modern B-segment hot hatch profile with "fang" front LED profile.
- **Details:** Roof air vent scoop, rear spoiler, rally mudflaps.
- **Livery:** Blue with Peugeot Sport white and yellow accents.

### 8. Hyundai i20 RX Supercar (`rally_hyundai_i20_rx`)
- **Silhouette:** Ultra-aggressive widebody rallycross supercar with flared wheel arches.
- **Details:** Front splitter, large roof scoop, multi-tier WRC/RX rear wing, red mudflaps.
- **Livery:** Sky Blue and Crimson Red Hyundai Motorsport livery.

### 9. Audi Sport Quattro S1 E2 (`rally_audi_sport_quattro_s1`)
- **Silhouette:** Iconic Group B short wheelbase, boxy flared arches, giant front snowplow splitter.
- **Details:** Massive dual-plane rear wing, roof cooling ducts, aggressive front canards.
- **Livery:** White and Yellow Audi Sport rally livery.

### 10. Toyota GR DKR Hilux T1+ (`rally_toyota_hilux_t1_plus`)
- **Silhouette:** Massive Dakar rally raid truck on 37-inch off-road tires with 350mm suspension travel.
- **Details:** High ground clearance, snorkel intake, spare tire rack in bed.
- **Livery:** Red, Black, and White Gazoo Racing Dakar livery.

### 11. Stadium Super Truck V8 (`rally_sst_super_truck`)
- **Silhouette:** High-riding trophy truck body on long-travel stadium suspension.
- **Details:** Open wheel wells, high suspension roll clearance, visible shock towers.
- **Livery:** Bright Orange and Black SST scheme.
