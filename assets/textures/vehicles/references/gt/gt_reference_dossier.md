# Gran Turismo & Endurance GT Reference Dossier

## Tier 1: GT4 Clubsport
### 1. Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 RS Clubsport (`gt_porsche_718_gt4`)
- **Silhouette:** Mid-engine compact fastback. Sloping front hood with dual NACA ducts.
- **Side Intakes:** Window quarter scoops replacing rear quarter glass, plus lower side intake pods.
- **Aero:** Carbon front splitter with side flics, tall swan-neck rear wing with curved endplates.
- **Livery:** Arctic Silver / GT Silver with black Clubsport graphics and yellow brake calipers.

### 2. BMW M4 GT4 (G82) (`gt_bmw_m4_gt4`)
- **Silhouette:** Front-engine muscular coupe with long sculpted hood and deep cooling vents.
- **Front Fascia:** Tall vertical twin-kidney grille silhouette and aggressive front splitter.
- **Aero:** High-mount carbon rear wing, deep rear diffuser, roof fins.
- **Livery:** Alpine White with BMW M tricolor stripes (Light Blue, Dark Blue, Red).

### 3. Aston Martin Vantage AMR GT4 (`gt_aston_vantage_gt4`)
- **Silhouette:** Low wide front-mid layout with classic Aston Martin shark nose and side strakes.
- **Aero:** Extended front splitter, full-width pedestal rear wing.
- **Livery:** Aston Martin Racing Green with Lime AMR accent stripes.

### 4. Toyota GR Supra GT4 EVO (`gt_toyota_supra_gt4`)
- **Silhouette:** Compact double-bubble roof, long hood, short rear overhang.
- **Aero:** Front canards, ducktail bootlid with high GT4 rear wing.
- **Livery:** Gazoo Racing Red, White, and Matte Black.

## Tier 2: GT3 Evo / FIA GT3
### 5. Porsche 911 GT3 R (992) (`gt_porsche_911_gt3r`)
- **Silhouette:** Rear-engine continuous teardrop slope from roofline to rear deck.
- **Front Hood:** Dual large extraction nostrils feeding radiator air over roof.
- **Aero:** Widebody fender louvers, massive carbon swan-neck rear wing, wide rear diffuser.
- **Livery:** White and Guards Red with black carbon accents.

### 6. Ferrari 296 GT3 (`gt_ferrari_296_gt3`)
- **Silhouette:** Low 120° V6 mid-engine layout, flying buttresses from roof to rear engine cover.
- **Front Fascia:** Pointed arrowhead nose with dual carbon dive planes / canards.
- **Aero:** Enormous swan-neck rear wing with low drag mainplane, underfloor venturi tunnels.
- **Livery:** Rosso Corsa with Giallo Modena yellow accents and race number plate.

### 7. Mercedes-AMG GT3 Evo (`gt_amg_gt3_evo`)
- **Silhouette:** Extra-long hood (front-mid 6.2L V8), cab-backward proportions.
- **Front Fascia:** Prominent Panamericana vertical grille, front dive planes.
- **Aero:** Large front wheel arch louvers, high rear GT wing, deep side exhausts.
- **Livery:** Selenite Matte Grey with Petronas Turquoise accents.

### 8. Audi R8 LMS GT3 Evo II (`gt_audi_r8_gt3_evo2`)
- **Silhouette:** Mid-engine wedge stance with signature carbon sideblades.
- **Roof:** Central roof ram-air scoop feeding the 5.2L V10 engine bay.
- **Aero:** Pedestal rear GT wing, rear diffuser fins.
- **Livery:** Mythos Black with Misano Red Audi Sport graphics.

## Tier 3: GT2 Biturbo
### 9. Porsche 911 GT2 RS Clubsport (`gt_porsche_911_gt2_rs`)
- **Silhouette:** Muscular 911 turbo body with large intercooler intakes in rear fenders.
- **Aero:** Large GT2 rear wing, Weissach carbon roof, dual central exhaust tips.
- **Livery:** GT Silver with carbon bonnet and red accents.

### 10. Brabham BT62 GT2 (`gt_brabham_bt62_gt2`)
- **Silhouette:** Ultralight carbon track special, razor-thin nose with deep hood pass-through scoop.
- **Aero:** Giant rear wing with swan-neck brackets, front and side dive planes.
- **Livery:** British Racing Green with historic Brabham Gold stripes.

### 11. Maserati MC20 GT2 (`gt_maserati_mc20_gt2`)
- **Silhouette:** Sculpted carbon monocoque with high front fender peaks.
- **Roof:** Prominent roof scoop for the Nettuno Twin-Turbo V6.
- **Aero:** Wide GT2 rear wing, rear extraction vents.
- **Livery:** Blu Corse with White and Italian Tricolore details.

### 12. Audi R8 LMS GT2 (`gt_audi_r8_gt2`)
- **Silhouette:** Extreme roof-mounted snorkel intake rising high above the cockpit.
- **Aero:** Widebody aerodynamic package, GT2 rear wing.
- **Livery:** Vegas Yellow with Dark Titanium accents.

## Tier 4: GT1 Legend
### 13. Porsche 911 GT1-98 (`gt_porsche_911_gt1_98`)
- **Silhouette:** Full carbon prototype monocoque, elongated low nose, low cockpit dome, long flat rear deck.
- **Aero:** Integrated wide rear wing spanning the full body width, front fender louvers.
- **Livery:** Mobil 1 White, Blue, and Red 1998 Le Mans winning scheme.

### 14. McLaren F1 GTR Longtail (`gt_mclaren_f1_gtr_lt`)
- **Silhouette:** Signature extended "Longtail" rear deck, central cockpit bubble with roof intake scoop.
- **Aero:** High rear wing on twin pylons, front chin splitter with dive planes.
- **Livery:** Papaya Orange and Carbon Black.

### 15. Mercedes-Benz CLK GTR (`gt_mercedes_clk_gtr`)
- **Silhouette:** Low wide GT1 body with iconic Mercedes grille, quad headlights, and cab-backward profile.
- **Aero:** Rear wing integrated with rear deck haunches.
- **Livery:** Silver Arrows metallic silver with D2 black accents.

### 16. Nissan R390 GT1 (`gt_nissan_r390_gt1`)
- **Silhouette:** Smooth flowing Japanese supercar silhouette, long tail, low twin headlights.
- **Aero:** Integrated high-downforce rear wing, side NACA ducts.
- **Livery:** Calsonic Blue and Red.

## Tier 5: Hypercar Prototype (LMH & LMDh)
### 17. Ferrari 499P LMH (`gt_ferrari_499p`)
- **Silhouette:** Closed cockpit prototype dome, low wedge nose with horizontal LED light bar.
- **Aero:** Central dorsal shark fin along the engine cover, innovative bi-plane rear wing.
- **Livery:** Rosso Le Mans with Giallo Modena yellow bands and #51/#50 livery.

### 18. Porsche 963 LMDh (`gt_porsche_963`)
- **Silhouette:** Multimatic chassis prototype, 4-point LED headlights in fenders.
- **Aero:** Large dorsal fin connected to high rear wing, low side pods with undercuts.
- **Livery:** White, Red, and Black Porsche Penske Motorsport scheme.

### 19. Toyota GR010 Hybrid (`gt_toyota_gr010`)
- **Silhouette:** High-nose LMH prototype with prominent front fender crests.
- **Aero:** Dorsal fin, wide rear wing, sculpted side intake pods.
- **Livery:** Matte Black, White, and Gazoo Racing Red.

### 20. Cadillac V-Series.R (`gt_cadillac_v_series_r`)
- **Silhouette:** Vertical blade headlights in front fenders, stealth fighter body creases.
- **Aero:** Prominent dorsal fin, wide rear wing, open diffuser tunnels.
- **Livery:** Electric Gold / Yellow with carbon black lower aero.
