# Extreme Off-Road & Karting Reference Dossier

## Extreme Off-Road & Arenas
### 1. Can-Am Maverick R Trophy Spec (`offroad_sand_rail_buggy`)
- **Silhouette:** Aggressive UTV / Sand Rail tube chassis, long-travel trailing arms.
- **Details:** Roof LED lightbar, rear whip antenna with neon orange safety pennant, rear paddle tires.
- **Livery:** Can-Am Gold/Yellow and Black.

### 2. Geiser Bros AWD Trophy Truck (`offroad_baja_trophy_truck`)
- **Silhouette:** Full-size Baja 1000 trophy truck on 40-inch desert tires.
- **Details:** Front high-clearance skid plate, roof LED lightbar, exposed dual rear spare tires, King bypass shocks.
- **Livery:** Blue and White desert racing livery.

### 3. Subaru WRX STI Ice Racer (`offroad_subaru_ice_racer`)
- **Silhouette:** Widebody Japanese sports sedan with tall STI rear wing.
- **Details:** Roof air vent scoop, studded ice tires with metal spikes, front bumper rally light pods.
- **Livery:** WR Blue Pearl with Gold racing wheels and yellow STI accents.

### 4. Mega Truck V8 Mud Slinger (`offroad_mega_mud_truck`)
- **Silhouette:** Super-tall monster lift kit, massive 48-inch agricultural chevron tractor tires.
- **Details:** Open tubular roll cage, supercharged engine blower sticking up through hood, high dual exhaust stacks.
- **Livery:** Moss Green and Mud Splatter graphics.

### 5. Grave Digger Spec Monster Jam (`offroad_grave_crusher`)
- **Silhouette:** Iconic 1950s panel van monster truck body on gargantuan 66-inch BKT tires.
- **Details:** Heavy tubular spaceframe, 4-link nitrogen suspension, supercharged V8 exhaust headers.
- **Livery:** Black with green flames and purple skull graphics.

## Karting & Micro-Racers
### 6. CRG Hero 60cc Cadet (`kart_crg_hero_60`)
- **Silhouette:** Compact junior sprint kart chassis, low black Nassau steering panel.
- **Details:** Rounded black side pod bumpers, exposed 60cc air-cooled engine cylinder head, small steering wheel.
- **Livery:** Black and Orange CRG factory kart colors.

### 7. Tony Kart Racer 401 RR OK (`kart_tony_kart_racer_ok`)
- **Silhouette:** Senior direct-drive sprint kart with aerodynamic OTK M10 bodywork pods.
- **Details:** Nassau steering panel, low floor tray, side aluminum radiator, exposed driver in racing suit.
- **Livery:** Signature OTK Green, White, and Red.

### 8. Birel ART KZ2 125cc Shifter (`kart_birel_art_kz2`)
- **Silhouette:** 6-speed shifter kart with aggressive Freeline aerodynamic pods.
- **Details:** Right-side sequential shifter lever, front brake master cylinder on steering wheel, large side radiator with silicone hoses.
- **Livery:** Red and White Birel ART factory racing livery.

### 9. Honda Mean Mower V2 Tuned (`kart_honda_mean_mower`)
- **Silhouette:** Tuned racing lawnmower with front tractor hood, high upright driver seat, and low chassis.
- **Details:** Side grass discharge chute, low-profile racing slicks, exposed Fireblade motorcycle engine exhaust.
- **Livery:** Honda Racing Red and White.

### 10. Anderson CS250 Twin GP Superkart (`kart_anderson_cs250`)
- **Silhouette:** Full-body aerodynamic carbon composite shell encapsulating the driver's lower body and legs.
- **Details:** Pointed aerodynamic nosecone, high cockpit wrap-around screen, Formula-style multi-element rear wing.
- **Livery:** Yellow and Carbon Black with race number.
