# Classic Heritage Circuit Catalog 🏎️🏛️

The **Classic Heritage** tracks form the foundational proving grounds of **TdRace** (revamped per Spec 055). Located in [`tracks/classic/`](../../tracks/classic), these 18 venues showcase foundational mechanics: asphalt apex clipping, high-risk intersection crossings, multi-surface transitions, dirt sliding, 2.5D elevation overpasses, and jump ramps across 6 motorsport disciplines.

> [!NOTE]
> Classic circuits serve as the universal arcade playground. They are available under the **Classic Motorsport** module and can be driven by any classic vehicle preset or imported into the Track Studio.

## 🔍 Featured Circuit: Coastal Grand Prix
* **File Location**: [`tracks/classic/gt_coastal_grand_prix.json`](../../tracks/classic/gt_coastal_grand_prix.json)
* **Surface Composition**: $100\%$ Asphalt with FIA Grade apex curbs.
* **Telemetry Hotspots**:
  * **Main Straight**: $410\text{ m}$ high-speed acceleration straight past the grandstands.
  * **Turn 1 (Plateau Climb)**: Elevation rise up to $+5.0\text{ m}$ overlooking the bay.
  * **Seaside Carousel**: $165^\circ$ parabolic right-hander testing lateral grip and sustained downforce.
  * **Bus-Stop Chicane**: Heavy threshold braking and curb-clipping weight transfer sequence before the pit straight.
