# Karting & Micro-Racers Roster 🏎️

The **Karting & Micro-Racers** module spans 5 tiers of grassroots and high-speed micro motorsport, featuring 15 authentically calibrated competitors from Tony Kart, CRG, Birel ART, Honda Racing, John Deere Custom, Viking Racing, Anderson, MS Kart, and VIPER. Characterized by zero suspension (flexible chassis frame roll), ultra-low CG, 1:1 direct steering ratios, and astronomical power-to-weight performance.
