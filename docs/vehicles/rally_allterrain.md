# Rallycross & All-Terrain Roster 🏎️

The **Rallycross & All-Terrain** module spans 5 tiers of multi-surface point-to-point and circuit rally racing, featuring 15 authentically calibrated competitors from Peugeot, Ford, Renault, Hyundai, Volkswagen, Audi, Lancia, Toyota, and Prodrive. Characterized by rapid-acceleration turbocharged engines, long-travel suspension damping, and explosive slide control across mixed gravel, dirt, and tarmac.
