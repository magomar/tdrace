# Extreme Off-Road & Stunt Arenas Roster 🏎️

The **Extreme Off-Road & Arenas** module spans 5 tiers of punishing rough-terrain and stadium competition, featuring 15 authentically calibrated competitors from Can-Am, Polaris, VW, Geiser Bros, Mason Motorsport, Subaru, Mitsubishi, and Monster Jam. Characterized by massive tire diameters, locked differentials, extreme ground clearance, and specialized surface dynamics for mud bogs, ice lakes, and sand dunes.
