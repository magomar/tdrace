# NASCAR Stock Car Racing Roster 🏎️

The **NASCAR Stock Car Racing** module spans 5 tiers of heavy-metal American oval and road course racing, featuring 15 authentically calibrated real-world competitors from Chevrolet, Ford, Dodge, and Toyota. Characterized by heavy steel tubular chassis, high-power naturally aspirated pushrod V8s, minimal downforce, and asymmetric oval setups.
